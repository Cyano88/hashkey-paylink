# Pocket by Hash PayLink

## Store title

Pocket by Hash PayLink

## Short description

Send, receive and manage USDC, payment requests and bank payouts.

## Full description

Pocket brings your supported USDC activity into one focused mobile experience.

Use Pocket to:

- view supported USDC balances;
- send USDC to a Pocket ID or wallet address;
- receive USDC and share wallet details;
- create, accept and pay supported payment requests;
- follow payment status in Activity and open receipts;
- request supported local-currency bank payouts where available;
- approve payments with a Pocket PIN and optional device biometrics; and
- receive selected payment, request, security and service notifications.

Pocket uses user-controlled wallet infrastructure. Hash PayLink does not ask for or store your seed phrase or private key.

Networks, currencies, providers, limits, fees, exchange rates and feature availability vary by location and may change. Blockchain transfers can be irreversible. Always verify the recipient, network, amount and displayed payment details before confirming.

## Listing guardrails

- Do not claim that Pocket is a bank, insured deposit account, licensed exchange, investment product or custodial wallet.
- Do not claim instant or guaranteed bank settlement.
- Do not advertise a feature until it is enabled and supportable in the submitted production build.
- Use screenshots with disposable accounts and redacted wallet, bank, email, support and transaction identifiers.
