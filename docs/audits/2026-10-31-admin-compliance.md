# Separate verification and compliance review — 2026-10-31

Business verification applications remain under Verification. Pending case escalations now have a dedicated Compliance navigation item, workspace selector, Pocket/Business source label, and scoped review count. The queue includes Compliance and Legal escalations; existing case permissions determine visibility and decision access. Resolved cases and completed reviews do not count.

No KYB decision, provider verification or test approval was performed. Personal KYC status remains visible in Businesses; this change does not add manual personal-KYC approval.

Validated: operations attention tests (including destination-specific reviewer counts, cross-workspace isolation and resolved-case exclusion), operations case authorization regressions, and Playwright desktop/mobile separation and workspace switching. Production build and live verification follow release.
