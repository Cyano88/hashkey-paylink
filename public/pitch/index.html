How the proposed reserve model works
Customer pays stablecoins→NGN / UGX pool pays locally→Compliant partner exchanges stablecoins→Local reserve is replenished

We plan to pre-fund local-currency payout pools, then regularly rebalance the stablecoins received through compliant banks or licensed liquidity partners that support the activity. This keeps local money available without making each customer wait for a fresh conversion. Partner agreements, reserve sizing, safeguarding and regulatory requirements must be in place before this model launches.

REVENUE MODEL

0.25%

Our standard platform fee per payment. On a $100 payment, that is $0.25 in gross platform fees.

Useful payments create repeat business.

People pay bills, merchants receive orders and developers bring their own users. Gross fees must cover provider, liquidity, network and operating costs; reserves support delivery and are not revenue.
