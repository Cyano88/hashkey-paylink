# Payment method marks

Original SVG artwork downloaded without modification from the official asset archives on 2026-11-02:

- Apple Pay: https://developer.apple.com/apple-pay/marketing/Apple-Pay-Mark.zip — `Apple Pay Mark/SVG/Apple_Pay_Mark_RGB_041619.svg`
- Google Pay: https://developers.google.com/static/pay/api/download-assets/Google-Pay-Acceptance.zip — `Google Pay Mark/google-pay-mark_800.svg`

Guidelines: https://developer.apple.com/apple-pay/marketing/ and https://developers.google.com/pay/api/web/guides/brand-guidelines

These are identity marks, not payment buttons or evidence of integration approval. Preserve aspect ratio, artwork, colors and clear space. Upcoming methods are disabled and explicitly labeled Coming soon. Stablecoins use a neutral coin-stack symbol because stablecoins are a category rather than one brand. Card, bank and wallet integrations require separate activation work.
